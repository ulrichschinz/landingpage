02General principles of processing

We process personal data following the principle of data minimisation — meaning only when it is necessary to provide this website or to handle your request, and where a legal basis under Art. 6 GDPR applies.

This website does without ad networks, social-media plugins and embedded third-party content. For reach measurement and usage analysis we use the service Venta AI — but only if you have consented beforehand. Without your consent the service is not loaded. Details and how to withdraw are set out in section 08.

03Server log files

When you access this website, our hosting provider automatically collects information that your browser transmits to the server. This includes:

  • IP address of the requesting device (anonymised or deleted after 7 days)
  • Date and time of the request
  • URL accessed or name of the requested file
  • HTTP status code and amount of data transferred
  • Browser used (user agent) and operating system
  • Referrer URL (previously visited page, if transmitted)

These data are technically required to deliver the website, ensure its stability and security, and to investigate attempted attacks. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (legitimate interest). Retention period: 7 days, after which the data are automatically deleted. No combination with other data sources takes place.

04Contact via email

If you contact us via the contact link or by email, your local email client opens. Data is transmitted to us exclusively by email — this website does not store any form input.

We process the data contained in your email (name, email address, company if applicable, content of your message) exclusively to respond to your enquiry and to handle any business relationship that may arise from it. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR (pre-contractual measures) or (f) (legitimate interest in effective communication). Retention period: until your request has been handled, then in accordance with statutory retention periods (typically 6 or 10 years if a business relationship arises), otherwise deletion after 12 months.

05Contact form (intro call)

When you use the booking form on this website, we process the following data:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Company (optional)
  • Brief description of your topic (optional)

Purpose: Processing your contact request and preparing an introductory call.
Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR (pre-contractual measures).
Transfer: Form data is transmitted via SMTP to our email service provider domainfactory GmbH, with whom a data processing agreement (DPA) pursuant to Art. 28 GDPR is in place. Servers are located in the EU.
Retention period: Form inputs are not stored persistently by the web server; data forwarded by email is handled as described in section 04.
Spam protection: We use a technical honeypot (a hidden form field) to detect automated spam submissions. No third-party CAPTCHA service is used.
Form analytics: If you have consented to the use of Venta AI (section 08), that service additionally records your interaction with this form — including the field contents you enter (name, email address, company, message) — and transmits them to Venta AI. Without your consent this does not happen at all; the form works regardless. Password and payment fields are technically excluded; this form does not contain such fields in any case.

08Cookies & analytics (Venta AI)

This website uses Venta AI, a service for reach measurement and analysis of usage behaviour. The provider is april.eleven GmbH, Am Kartoffelgarten 14, 81671 Munich, Germany (Munich Local Court, HRB 279522).

When you access this website, a script provided by the provider is loaded (cdn.getventa.ai) which collects usage data and transmits it to api.getventa.ai. In particular, the following are processed:

  • pages viewed, time on page, clicks, scroll and download events
  • referrer (the page you came from) and campaign parameters in the URL you opened
  • technical details about your browser, operating system, device type and screen resolution
  • your IP address — the provider uses it, among other things, to identify the company from whose network the visit originates (company-level attribution)
  • your entries in forms on this website, including the field contents (see section 05)
  • a pseudonymous device identifier stored in the cookie described below

Cookies and storage technologies used

Venta AI stores information on your device or reads information from it:

  • Cookie venta_device_id — pseudonymous device identifier, lifetime 365 days, set on the domain .agentic-reach.com
  • Local storage — venta_device_id and venta_session (each with a corresponding _expires entry): device identifier plus session and usage data, retained for up to 365 days
  • Session storage (prefixed radar_sn_) — session data, deleted after 24 hours at the latest or when you close the browser tab

A session ends automatically after 30 minutes of inactivity. If cookies from other services (e.g. HubSpot, Intercom, Meta/Facebook) are present in your browser, Venta AI may also read and transmit those identifiers. We do not use any such services ourselves.

Legal basis

Storing information on, and reading information from, your device is based on your consent pursuant to Section 25(1) TDDDG. The subsequent processing of the personal data collected in this way is based on Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (consent).

On your first visit to this website we ask for your consent via a notice at the bottom of the screen. Until you have agreed, the Venta AI script is not loaded — no data is transmitted to the provider and no cookies or other entries are stored on your device. If you decline, the service remains disabled permanently; the website is fully usable without it.

You may withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future — via the Cookie settings link at the bottom of every page. On withdrawal, the cookies and storage entries set by Venta AI are deleted. The lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal remains unaffected.

Your decision itself is stored in your browser's local storage under the entry ar_consent. This entry is necessary to give effect to your choice and therefore does not itself require consent (Section 25(2)(2) TDDDG). It contains only your decision and its timestamp, and is not transmitted.

Recipients, retention and transfers to third countries

A data processing agreement pursuant to Art. 28 GDPR is in place with the provider. The script is delivered, and the data transmitted, via content delivery and infrastructure services that also operate servers outside the EU; a transfer to third countries — in particular to the USA — can therefore not be ruled out. Any such transfer takes place on the basis of the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or an adequacy decision. For retention periods and sub-processors, please refer to the provider at getventa.ai.

Objection and deletion

The simplest route is to withdraw consent via Cookie settings at the bottom of the page — this stops the tracking and deletes the entries that were set. Independently of that, you can block or delete cookies and website data for this domain in your browser, enable your browser's tracking protection, or use a script blocker.

We use neither advertising networks nor social-media pixels. Session recording and screen capture are disabled in the Venta AI configuration we use.

09Your rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access (Art. 15 GDPR) — to the data we process about you
  • Rectification (Art. 16 GDPR) — correction of inaccurate data
  • Erasure (Art. 17 GDPR) — unless retention obligations apply
  • Restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
  • Data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)
  • Objection (Art. 21 GDPR) — to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Withdrawal (Art. 7 (3) GDPR) — of any consent given, with effect for the future

To exercise your rights, an informal email to hello@agentic-reach.com is sufficient.

10Right to lodge a complaint

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority if you believe that the processing of your personal data violates the GDPR (Art. 77 GDPR). Competent is the authority of the federal state of your residence or of the alleged infringement.
Competent for the controller: Bavarian State Office for Data Protection Supervision (BayLDA), Promenade 18, 91522 Ansbach — www.lda.bayern.de.
